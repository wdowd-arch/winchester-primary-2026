Both seats are open, and both are decided across several communities. Each race below counts every city and town in its district, reported as each community posts its totals. Winchester is broken out precinct by precinct because that is where our reporters are.

Winchester is split between the two districts: precincts 4, 5, 6 and 7 vote in the 2nd Middlesex; precincts 1, 2, 3 and 8 vote in the 5th Middlesex.

Each community's totals are entered by Winchester News as that community posts its results. Winchester's own precincts are counted by our reporters at the polls. A blank means a community has not reported yet, not that it recorded no votes.

Unofficial and subject to correction. Official results are certified by the Secretary of the Commonwealth. The 2nd Middlesex district covers Somerville, Medford, Cambridge wards 9–11 and Winchester precincts 4–7; the 5th Middlesex covers Malden, Melrose, Reading, Stoneham, Wakefield and Winchester precincts 1, 2, 3 and 8. The general election is Tuesday, Nov. 3, 2026.
